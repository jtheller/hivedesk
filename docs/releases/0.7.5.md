# 0.7.5

- Click directly in the top-left screen corner to activate the compact quick-swap
  preview. Its click and hover area now extends through the top/left visual inset;
  no diagonal movement into the card or animation wait is needed.
- Keep the existing previous-computer selection, local-desktop return, downward
  expansion into the full Hive list, and card drag/reorder behavior.
- Add isolated native UI coverage for exact-corner local/remote/previous-computer
  clicks at 100%, 150% and 200% scaling, cancelled presses, outside releases, and
  downward expansion. No input is injected on the user's working desktop.
