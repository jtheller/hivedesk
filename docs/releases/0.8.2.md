# 0.8.2

- Bind video UDP and generated path-probe sockets to the local IPv4 address of
  their authenticated TCP connection. Multi-address machines must reply from
  the address the peer actually connected to; wildcard UDP binding could choose
  another address and fail strict endpoint validation. Peer/port checks and
  encryption remain intact, with no network-topology exception or new setting.
- Exercise real encrypted full-duplex transfers between distinct local loopback
  addresses, including receive credit, lost-path recovery and bounded shutdown.
- Extend read-only diagnostics with negotiated target/cap, capture-wait time and
  separate connection/stream-request timestamps.

The fix follows a real VI trace: TCP connected to its address ending in .21,
while generated UDP replies arrived from its address ending in .20. Those
datagrams reached the socket but were correctly rejected by the endpoint check.
This was local source-address selection, not insufficient bandwidth.

Validation: 43/43 native checks, managed update/GPU-policy checks and real WinUI
light/dark checks passed. Final focused checks establish the complete transport
and generated probe over a secondary loopback address and require UDP, rather
than accepting a silent TCP fallback. The distinct-address duplex/credit/loss
test also passed. A hidden HEVC 4:4:4 UDP loopback with delayed feedback delivered
535/535 encoded frames with no sender rejection. Live 0.8.1 DEVR observations
and their measurement limits are recorded in TRANSPORT-VALIDATION.md.
