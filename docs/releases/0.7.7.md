# 0.7.7

- Keep reading desktop-service activation replies and diagnostics while video
  waits for network send credit. Return that credit on an independent worker,
  with one pending return and cancellation/join before pipe reuse. Previously a
  slow transfer could block the same reader beyond the 2-second warm-promotion
  deadline even though both the service and network were still progressing.
- Extend the installed, input-free warm/Peek broker checks to hold video credit
  through activation, verify continuing diagnostics without premature frames,
  resume a fresh IDR, stop while blocked, and reuse the bridge for another lease.
- Log sender-side TCP windows, RTT, in-flight bytes and cumulative retransmission
  counters for writes older than 2 seconds, at most once every 5 seconds. This
  also covers warm/Peek sessions, without recording desktop or input contents.

DEVR investigation remains open: 0.7.6 authenticated and decoded a 2560x1440
HEVC 4:4:4 first picture after about 12 seconds. Video reception was approximately
0.8 Mbps; a separate SMB file read measured 0.57 Mbps. A following picture then
exceeded the transit budget, and the 2-second recovery deadline expired before
the much larger replacement IDR arrived. This release fixes the confirmed local
broker blocking defect and supplies missing sender evidence; it does not claim
to solve that low throughput or validate continuous DEVR playback.
