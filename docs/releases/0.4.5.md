# 0.4.5

Fix the reproduced Connecting stall when switching directly between computers
after opening Hive, including warmed windowed drops.

The viewer consumed the DXGI frame-latency signal before checking its prepared
picture. If that picture had expired while Hive was open, or had been superseded
by an IDR, it discarded the picture and waited for another signal without ever
calling Present. Live video could keep decoding at 100 FPS while presentation
remained at zero. A successful Present was needed to replenish the admission it
had already consumed.

Retain the acquired presentation admission across discarded/replaced pictures
and unsuccessful Present retries. Consume it only when Present succeeds. This
preserves the one-frame presentation queue and latest-picture behavior.

Validation:

- Release native/WinUI builds completed; all 34 CTest checks and the explicit
  real-DWM warm-presentation regression passed.
- Reproduced twice on the installed 0.4.4 agent while switching from a live VI
  stream to 6RE. Video reception and decoding continued near 100 FPS, dedicated
  input was ready, and presentation stayed at zero until the 16-second startup
  deadline caused a cold retry. A live thread stack located the presenter in
  the DXGI availability wait; renderer retirement completed normally.
- Extended the generated real-DWM presentation check: prepare a warm image,
  let it age for 300 ms, activate, and deliver fresh images after another 100 ms.
  The old implementation fails; the repaired implementation presents. Preserve
  a consistent timestamp basis and monotonically increasing frame sequences in
  this fixture.
- Ran the repaired agent against the existing VI and 6RE computers: 30 completed
  alternating selections, both fullscreen and windowed, then eight bursts of
  five rapid selections/cancellations. All 38 final targets became ready, with
  no restart or startup timeout. Ordinary selections took approximately
  0.21-0.29 seconds; rapid-burst final selections took 1.20-2.17 seconds including
  their final 100 ms panel dwell. These are application ready observations, not
  input-to-photon measurements or a claim about all network conditions.

No remote keyboard or mouse packets were injected by the test driver. It drove
only Hive's local switcher messages. Tests returned to the local desktop.
Codec, bitrate policy, physical refresh rates, protocol and prewarming scope
are unchanged. The fix is needed on the computer displaying remote video.
