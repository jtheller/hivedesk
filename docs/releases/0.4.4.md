# 0.4.4

Harden visible-peer prewarming and rapid viewer replacement after reports of
Connecting stalls during windowed drops and fast selections.

- Warm viewers decode and prepare their first picture while their presenter is
  suspended. Selection explicitly activates presentation; hidden warm windows
  no longer attempt Present or retry an occluded swapchain. Cancellation and
  decoder failure wake the suspended presenter before joining its thread.
- Retain one immutable window lease for warm validation and viewer assignment.
  Re-reading the current UI surface could associate an old warmed decoder with
  a newer window when the same peer was selected again during startup.
- Never join viewer teardown while holding the selection/input intent lock.
- Bound each startup attempt: eight seconds without byte progress, sixteen
  seconds total even with continued traffic but no usable picture/input channel.
  Existing reconnect and Escape/new-selection cancellation remain available.
- Log recovery reasons and renderer retirement boundaries; name the command,
  decode, mapping and presentation threads. Keep a local linker map for resolving
  future installed-build stacks without shipping private symbols.

Add a full-App regression on a noninteractive window station: three generated
encrypted peers, all-visible prewarm, warm promotion, repeated selection,
windowed drops, cancellation and shutdown. The test checks that actual stream
starts and warm promotions occurred. Extend real NVDEC tests with unselected
presentation gating and cancellation before activation, and bound startup
progress in the reconnect tests.

These changes address confirmed lifecycle gaps. The reported permanent hang has
not yet been reproduced under the isolated tests or captured in a live blocked
thread stack; do not treat this release as proof that every hang is eliminated.
HEVC444 settings, physical refresh rates and steady-state video pacing are unchanged.

Validation: 34 CTest checks pass; final reconnect, shell/NVDEC and full-App checks
pass again. The explicit warm-only visible-DWM check verifies prepared pixels do
not Present until activation and then submit successfully without input or focus
changes. The broader pixel-based animation probe failed its card-color assertion
on this interactive desktop before reaching video; no composition/UI animation
code is changed in this release.
